# Privacy Policy — Haramain Monitor

Haramain Monitor is designed to be **100% offline and private**.

- **No network access:** The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission and contains no network, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, or advertising code.
- **Microphone audio:** Live microphone audio is processed in real time in memory on your device to feed your headphones.
- **Recordings:** Recordings are saved locally in the app's private storage on your device (or to a local folder you explicitly pick via Android's Storage Access Framework). Recordings never leave your device unless you explicitly tap **Share** or **Export**.
- **Settings & Practice Log:** Preferences, custom presets, and practice history are stored locally in Android DataStore on your device.

If you have questions or open-source inquiries, please open an issue in the project repository.
